# 🛡️ SwaShakti
### AI-Powered Women's Safety Operating System

> **Empowering Every Journey with Intelligent Safety.**

SwaShakti is an AI-powered Women's Safety Operating System designed to provide **proactive protection**, **intelligent route planning**, **real-time emergency response**, and **community-driven safety intelligence**. Instead of reacting after an emergency occurs, SwaShakti continuously analyzes environmental conditions, predicts potential risks, and assists users in making safer decisions through Explainable AI.

Built for **Build for Good 2026**.

---

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Most existing safety applications only become useful **after** an emergency begins.

Users often need to:
- Unlock their phone
- Search for emergency contacts
- Dial emergency numbers
- Explain their location
- Wait for help

During high-stress situations, these actions can cost valuable time.

SwaShakti minimizes these barriers by combining AI, geospatial intelligence, multilingual communication, and community safety data into a single intelligent safety platform.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

SwaShakti functions as an **AI Safety Operating System** that helps users:

- Prevent unsafe situations
- Navigate through safer routes
- Monitor journeys intelligently
- Detect emergencies automatically
- Coordinate emergency response
- Assist family members
- Support first responders
- Learn from community safety reports

# ✨ Key Features

## 🧠 AI Safety Score Engine
- Calculates the safest route instead of the shortest route
- Generates AI-based Safety Scores
- Uses:
  - Crowd density
  - Time of day
  - Lighting conditions
  - Community reports
  - Safe public locations
  - Nearby hospitals and police booths
- Displays Explainable AI reasoning and confidence scores

---

## 🗺️ AI Safety Map
- Interactive AI-powered map
- Safe / Moderate / Unsafe route visualization
- Run-To Safe Haven navigation
- Nearby:
  - Police Booths
  - Hospitals
  - Metro Stations
  - Public Safe Zones

---

## 🚨 Smart SOS
One-tap emergency response that:

- Captures live GPS
- Generates live tracking link
- Alerts emergency contacts
- Starts Guardian Mode
- Activates Evidence Vault

---

## 📱 Hardware Emergency Trigger
Emergency activation without unlocking the phone.

Supports:
- Shake phone (3 times)
- Rapid power button presses

Automatically starts SOS workflow.

---

## 🚖 AI Guardian Mode
Smart journey monitoring that:

- Tracks route
- Detects deviations
- Monitors long stops
- Identifies unsafe areas
- Automatically checks user safety
- Triggers SOS if no response is received

---

## 📍 AI Route Deviation Detection
AI continuously compares:

Expected Route

↓

Current Route

↓

Risk Level

↓

Emergency Decision

Automatically detects suspicious travel behavior.

## 👥 Community Risk Intelligence

- Harassment hotspots
- Broken streetlights
- Unsafe roads
- Suspicious activity

AI learns from reports to generate dynamic safety heatmaps.
